## What "AI-friendly" really means

LLM-powered search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews) ranks using roughly the same signals as Google, with three notable additions:

1. **`llms.txt`** — a 2024-vintage standard adopted by Cloudflare, Vercel, and most major SaaS sites. AI engines read it as a curated fact-sheet about your product. **Done.**
2. **Clean, structured JSON-LD** — when an LLM extracts facts about your product, it reads JSON-LD first because it's already machine-readable. **Done.**
3. **External mentions** — AI engines cite what other sites already cite. The blog-post and directory work above is what gets you into the citation graph.

There is no guaranteed path to LLM citation. The above gives you a fair shot; the off-page work (directories, mentions, press) is what actually moves the needle.
